# Future scenario contract

A Future Maintainer Tournament scenario records opaque identity, provenance,
base revision, target and preservation constraints, equal execution budgets,
floor tests, independent probes, contamination controls, change-surface and
reversibility measures, and raw artifact references.

Selection applies hard floors, removes dominated candidates, constructs the
Pareto frontier, and only then applies organization risk policy. A weighted
aggregate score cannot override a failed hard constraint.
