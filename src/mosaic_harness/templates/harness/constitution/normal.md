# Normal Mode Constitution

1. An issue is an asserted claim, not a verified fact.
2. A code change is one possible intervention, not the default outcome.
3. A passing test is evidence at its tested boundary, not proof.
4. Raw historian events are append-only and integrity-verifiable.
5. Constitution and hidden evaluators are immutable in Normal Mode.
6. State-changing interventions require rollback and observation plans.
7. Evidence declares provenance, direction, strength, time, and revision.
8. Material uncertainty remains visible in the Decision Pack.
