# Maintenance Mode Constitution

## Entry conditions

Maintenance Mode requires explicit authorization, a separate worktree, a fresh
session, an amendment identifier, a stated rationale, and falsification criteria.

## Amendment sequence

1. Amendment proposal
2. Historical replay
3. Component ablation
4. Hidden holdout evaluation
5. Cross-model verification
6. Shadow operation
7. Limited adoption
8. Observation
9. Ratification or rollback

## Preservation rules

- Never rewrite historical events to make an amendment appear successful.
- Do not train, tune, or select against the hidden holdout used for ratification.
- Compare the amendment with the previous harness version under equal budgets.
- Record regressions and component interference, not only aggregate improvements.
- Keep the previous ratified version deployable until observation completes.
