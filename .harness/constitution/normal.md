# Normal Mode Constitution

## Purpose

Normal Mode investigates application behavior and evaluates proposed
interventions under the currently ratified constitution.

## Hard rules

1. An issue is an asserted claim, not a verified fact.
2. A code change is one possible intervention, not the default outcome.
3. A passing test is evidence at its tested boundary, not proof of correctness.
4. Raw historian events must be append-only and integrity-verifiable.
5. Constitution, permission policy, memory policy, and hidden evaluators are
   immutable in Normal Mode.
6. Every state-changing intervention needs a rollback trigger and an observation
   capable of revealing that the intervention is wrong.
7. Evidence must declare provenance, strength, direction, time, and applicable
   repository revision.
8. Material uncertainty must remain visible in the Decision Pack.

## Completion

A case is complete only when its accepted outcome is supported by the recorded
evidence, target constraints are satisfied, and preservation constraints remain
satisfied. Moving risk to another system or hiding uncertainty is not resolution.
